# Learning Contract: Team workflow and technical communication

Student: Taylor Sanders
Date: November 12, 2026
Target review date: November 20, 2026

## Primary competency

My primary competency is collaboration and technical communication. For this contract, I am targeting only these parts of that competency:

- Break work into a useful issue, task, or milestone with a clear definition of done.
- Communicate progress, questions, blockers, and integration needs early.
- Review another person's work with specific, respectful, actionable feedback.
- Record important decisions, alternatives considered, and reasons for the final choice.
- Integrate team changes while maintaining quality and ownership of your contribution.

I am not claiming that this contract completes the full collaboration and technical communication competency. I am focusing on the parts of the competency that are most important for working with a project team and communicating clearly during development.

## My starting point

From my prior contracts, I already know how to:

- Build and improve my Packaging Design Gallery with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Explain the choices I made in my project and show evidence of what works
- Review my own progress and identify what still needs improvement
- Use peer feedback to make my work stronger

I still need to improve:

- Breaking my work into smaller tasks with a specific definition of done
- Communicating progress and blockers in a way that helps others know what I need
- Giving feedback that is clear, respectful, and actionable
- Documenting the reason behind important technical decisions
- Handling project updates or changes without losing quality or ownership of my contribution

This contract is a good next step because the project is already developed enough that I can show how I work with the code, explain decisions, and communicate like a teammate rather than just as a solo developer.

## My learning plan

I will continue working on my Packaging Design Gallery and use it as the project context for this contract. I will focus on the communication and teamwork skills that are needed when a project grows beyond just building a page on my own.

I will practice in this order:

1. Review my current work and break it into a small set of tasks with a clear definition of done.
2. Create a short task or milestone plan for my next improvements and identify what evidence will show the work is complete.
3. Write a brief progress update that includes what I have finished, what I am working on, and any blockers or questions I have.
4. Review a classmate's project, code, or design work and provide specific, respectful feedback that can actually be used.
5. Record one important technical decision I made, including alternatives I considered and the reason I chose the final option.
6. Practice integrating a change or update into my project without losing quality or breaking the page.

My first milestone, due November 20, 2026, is a short task plan and communication record showing that I understand how to scope my work, explain my status, and document an important decision.

## Evidence of learning

I will submit or demonstrate:

- A short progress update or status note that explains what I finished, what I am working on, and any blockers
- A review I gave to another person that includes specific and respectful feedback
- A short decision record explaining an important technical choice, the alternatives, and the reason I selected the final approach

I will know I am ready for review when:

- My tasks are clearly scoped and practical to complete
- My communication explains progress and blockers in a useful way
- My feedback is specific, respectful, and actionable
- I can explain a project decision and why it was the best fit
- I can show that I maintained quality while integrating updates or changes

## Support and risks

The help I may need is:

- Feedback on whether my task breakdown is clear and realistic
- A peer review to tell me if my communication notes are easy to understand

My main risk is trying to make the contract too broad instead of focusing on a few clear communication tasks. If that happens, I will narrow the scope to one milestone, one decision record, and one communication/review example so the evidence stays strong and specific.

## AI use, if applicable

I may use AI to help me:

- Organize my work into a clearer milestone list
- Improve the wording of a progress update or review comment
- Brainstorm a concise way to document a technical decision
- Check whether my communication is clear and professional

I will not ask AI to decide the final content without me understanding the reasoning behind it. If I use AI, I will record the question I asked, decide which suggestions to keep or reject, and verify the final result by checking that it matches my actual project and my own explanation.
